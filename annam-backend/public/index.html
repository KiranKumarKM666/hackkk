Food Safety Rules & Non-Negotiables

Annam's safety engine rejects overdue food at source and guarantees recipient protection.

Hard Deadlines:
  • Safe-until times are strictly computed from official health regulations.
  • Donors may only shorten safe-until, never extend it.
  • Any post that cannot reach a receiver with a 10-minute safety buffer is systematically refused.
