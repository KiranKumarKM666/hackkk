Good food belongs on a table

Food donations can connect surplus and pantry staples with people who can use them. Whether you're an individual, a local business, or a community group, there's a way to take part.

Make your donation count

Before you donate, check with your local food program about what it can accept. Unopened pantry staples and fresh produce may be useful, depending on local needs and storage capacity.

Everyone can help

You can contribute food, help organize a collection, or share opportunities with friends and classmates. Small acts of generosity can bring a community together.
